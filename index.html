ACCOUNT ACCESS

Staff & access

Create accounts directly. Your role controls which account types and campuses you can manage.

Create an account

A temporary password is generated after account creation. Copy it and share it privately with the account holder. The password is not saved in the database.

Account creation permissions

  • Institution owner: all roles.
  • Institution administrator: campus administrators, lecturers and students.
  • Campus administrator: lecturers and students in their assigned campus.
  • Lecturer: students in their assigned campus.

Use each person’s real email address. They can use “Forgot password?” if they need to reset the temporary password.
