One connected workflow

More than a sign-in sheet.

Give your institution a structured way to open sessions, verify student attendance and review records—without relying on scattered paper registers.

▦

QR and rotating attendance codes

Lecturers open sessions with a QR code and six-digit attendance code. Session codes rotate periodically to help limit reuse of old check-in details.

⌖

Location-aware verification

Use GPS location and the institution’s configured attendance radius as part of the student check-in process.

▣

Student device binding

Bind a student account to its registered device. If a device needs to change, an authorised institution administrator can manage the reset.

⌂

Campus and course-unit control

Organise attendance around campuses, programmes, classes and course units so students see sessions relevant to their enrolment.

▤

Attendance reports

Review attendance records and generate downloadable PDF and CSV reports to support follow-up and record keeping.

✉

Parent SMS notifications

Institutions can configure Africa’s Talking to send parent attendance notifications. SMS provider charges and delivery depend on the institution’s provider account.

Which institutions can use InterCampus?

InterCampus is designed for universities, colleges, schools and other educational institutions that need to organise attendance across classes, programmes, course units or campuses.

How much does it cost?

The listed rate is UGX 250 per active registered student per month, following the 14-day trial. Your institution dashboard shows the active student count and subscription amount due.

Do students need an account?

Yes. Students use institution-created or approved student accounts to access eligible open sessions and submit attendance. The institution provides the Institution ID and account invitation details.

Can a student check in from anywhere?

Attendance check-in uses the session’s code or QR together with GPS location verification and the institution’s configured attendance radius. Device binding also helps restrict an account to its registered device.

Can parents receive attendance SMS messages?

Institutions can configure parent SMS notifications using their Africa’s Talking account. The institution is responsible for its SMS provider configuration and charges; provider acceptance does not guarantee handset delivery.

How do we get started?

An authorised representative creates the institution account, sets up the institution and campuses, and then adds the relevant administrators, lecturers and students. Start from the institution portal.
