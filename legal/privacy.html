InterCampus Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-08 · Operator: SSEMWANGA JOSHUA, Founder and Operator of InterCampus · Contact: jowicode@gmail.com

This policy explains how InterCampus Attendance handles personal data when an educational institution creates a workspace and when its staff and learners use the service. InterCampus is operated by an individual. It is not operated by BULENGA CHRIST LOVE MINISTRIES LIMITED.

Roles and responsibilities

The educational institution decides why and how it uses attendance and education records and is generally the data controller for those institutional records. InterCampus provides the software and processes data on the institution's documented instructions as a service provider/data processor, except for limited account, billing, security and service-operation information for which the operator determines the purpose.

Information processed

Depending on features enabled, information may include names, email addresses, phone numbers, institution and campus identifiers, roles, class/programme/course-unit enrolments, student or admission numbers, profile photographs, date of birth and gender where collected by the institution, parent/guardian contact details, invitation and account status, device identifiers, attendance timestamps, session codes, and GPS/location information when a session requires location verification. Authentication credentials are handled by Firebase Authentication; InterCampus does not store users' plaintext passwords.

Purposes

Information is used to create and secure accounts, connect users to the correct institution, administer campuses and course enrolments, send invitations, operate attendance sessions, verify attendance using configured controls, produce reports, prevent misuse, troubleshoot, and maintain the service. Institutions must decide which fields and controls are necessary and communicate them to their users.

Location and device data

GPS coordinates are requested only when the institution enables location verification for an attendance session. Device identifiers are used for account/device binding and attendance integrity. Users should be shown the relevant permission prompt by their device/browser. Do not enable these controls unless the institution has a lawful basis and has informed affected users.

Service providers and international processing

The service currently uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Firebase Hosting. Google may process information in locations determined by the configured Firebase resources and its service architecture. InterCampus does not promise that all information is stored in Uganda. The institution and operator must assess applicable cross-border transfer requirements and actual provider locations before production use. Provider terms and privacy information are available from Firebase privacy and security information.

Sharing

Information is made available to authorised users within the same institution according to their roles and campus permissions. It may be processed by infrastructure providers, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and users. InterCampus does not sell student personal data.

Retention and deletion

The institution is responsible for setting an appropriate retention period for its academic and attendance records and for instructing InterCampus when records should be deleted or exported. Account, security and transaction records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, dispute handling and legal obligations. The institution must not retain records indefinitely without a documented need.

Security

InterCampus uses role-based access controls, institution separation, Firebase authentication and database security rules. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Institutions must protect administrator accounts, promptly remove departed users, and report suspected incidents to jowicode@gmail.com.

Rights and requests

Individuals should normally contact their institution first about access, correction, objection, deletion or other rights relating to institutional attendance records. The institution can contact InterCampus for assistance. For InterCampus account/service questions, email jowicode@gmail.com. Applicable rights depend on the law governing the processing.

Children and learners

Institutions must ensure they have authority and a lawful basis to provide learner information, including information about children, and must provide appropriate notices to learners and parents/guardians where required.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in a new policy version and users may be asked to acknowledge the updated notice before continuing.
