InterCampus Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-13.1 · Operator: SSEMWANGA JOSHUA, Founder and Operator of InterCampus · Privacy and security contact: jowicode@gmail.com

This policy explains how InterCampus Attendance handles personal data when an educational institution creates a workspace and when its staff and learners use the service. It is intended to help institutions and users understand the data involved, the safeguards applied and the limits of those safeguards.

1. Roles and responsibilities

The educational institution decides why and how it uses attendance and education records and is generally the data controller for those institutional records. InterCampus provides the software and processes those records on the institution's documented instructions as a service provider/data processor, except for limited account administration, security and service-operation information for which the operator determines the purpose. The institution must provide its own required notices and establish a lawful basis for processing.

2. Information processed

Depending on features enabled and information supplied by the institution, information may include names, email addresses, phone numbers, institution and campus identifiers, roles, class/programme/course-unit enrolments, student or admission numbers, profile photographs, date of birth and gender where collected, parent/guardian contact details, invitation and account status, device identifiers, attendance timestamps, session codes and GPS/location information when a session requires location verification. Authentication credentials are handled by Firebase Authentication; InterCampus does not store users' plaintext passwords.

Institutions should collect only fields needed for a clear educational or operational purpose and should avoid placing sensitive or unrelated information in free-text fields.

3. Purposes and use limitation

Information is used to create and secure accounts, connect users to the correct institution, administer campuses and course enrolments, send invitations, operate attendance sessions, apply configured attendance checks, produce reports, prevent misuse, troubleshoot and maintain the service. InterCampus does not sell student personal data or use institutional attendance records to target advertising.

4. Location and device information

GPS coordinates are requested when the institution enables location verification for an attendance session and the user attempts attendance check-in. Device identifiers support account/device binding and attendance integrity. Location accuracy and device identifiers are technical signals; browser-based values can be affected or falsified by device settings, software or network conditions and must not be treated as conclusive proof. Institutions must explain these checks in advance, assess necessity and proportionality, and provide a fair review route for disputed results.

5. Access controls and security safeguards

InterCampus applies Firebase Authentication, institution-scoped database access rules, role- and campus-based permission checks, restrictions on sensitive session operations, and security-focused hosting headers. Database rules are designed to limit users to records permitted by their institution role and assignment. The original session creator is the only user permitted to close that session. Security tests are used to check important access-control behaviour.

These safeguards reduce risk but do not guarantee that every attack, account compromise, software defect or unauthorised disclosure can be prevented. Access also depends on accurate role assignments and secure user devices. Institutions should use strong unique passwords, protect administrator accounts, avoid shared accounts, promptly disable departed users and review access periodically.

6. Service providers and international processing

The service uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Firebase Hosting, and Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1 for billing operations and subscription records. Checkout and payment verification are provided through JJuma Global and payment partners made available by JJuma. These providers may process information in locations outside Uganda under their own service arrangements. InterCampus does not promise that all information is stored in Uganda. Institutions must assess applicable cross-border transfer requirements and provider locations before production use. See Firebase privacy and security information.

7. Subscription billing and payment information

When an institution uses paid subscription features, InterCampus processes billing information such as the institution identifier and name, active registered-student count used to calculate the subscription charge, subscription and trial status, billing period and renewal dates, selected checkout currency, amount due, exchange-rate information used for currency conversion, transaction/reference identifiers, payment status and payment timestamps. The billing service stores subscription and transaction records in Cloudflare D1 and uses Cloudflare Workers to provide billing, entitlement checks and payment-webhook handling. The billing service does not intentionally collect or store full payment-card numbers, card security codes, mobile-money PINs or payment-provider passwords. Payment credentials and payment method details are handled by the payment provider and its payment partners.

Checkout is initiated through JJuma Global. JJuma and the payment gateways or payment partners it makes available process the payment under their own terms and privacy notices. Payment confirmation is received through a signed provider webhook and checked against the expected transaction amount and currency before a subscription is activated. InterCampus receives and retains transaction identifiers, references, amount, currency and payment status needed to reconcile payments; it does not receive a user's full card credentials through this integration. Institutions should consult JJuma's current privacy information for details of its own processing and onward payment-provider arrangements.

8. Sharing and disclosure

Information is made available to authorised users within the same institution according to their roles and campus permissions. It may also be processed by infrastructure providers, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. InterCampus does not make institutional records public by design and does not sell personal data. Institutions are responsible for ensuring that exports and reports are shared only with authorised recipients.

9. Retention, export and deletion

The institution determines and documents appropriate retention periods for its academic and attendance records and instructs InterCampus when records should be exported or deleted. The current service does not promise automatic expiry or deletion of every category of institutional record. Account, security and service records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for operation, dispute handling and legal obligations. Institutions must not retain records indefinitely without a documented need. Deletion may be subject to technical limitations, backup cycles and legal retention duties.

10. Data incidents

If InterCampus becomes aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting institutional data, it will notify the institution's designated contact without undue delay and share information reasonably available to support assessment and response. Users who suspect unauthorised access, loss or disclosure should promptly contact their institution and email jowicode@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in an incident report.

11. Rights and privacy requests

Individuals should normally contact their institution first about access, correction, objection, deletion or other rights relating to institutional attendance records, because the institution determines the purposes and lawful basis. The institution can contact InterCampus for reasonable assistance. For InterCampus account or service questions, email jowicode@gmail.com. Applicable rights and response periods depend on the law governing the processing.

12. Children and learners

Institutions must ensure they have authority and a lawful basis to provide learner information, including information about children, and must provide age-appropriate notices and parent/guardian information where required. Institutions should not collect a child's information unless it is necessary and authorised.

13. User responsibilities

  • Keep login credentials private and use a secure device.
  • Report suspected account compromise or incorrect access promptly.
  • Do not attempt to access another institution's or another user's records.
  • Review institutional privacy notices and ask the institution how attendance decisions can be challenged.

14. Changes

Material changes will be reflected in a new policy version. Where appropriate, users or institution representatives may be asked to acknowledge the updated notice before continuing.
