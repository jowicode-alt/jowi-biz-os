Institutional Data Processing Agreement

Effective 8 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-08 · Contact: jowicode@gmail.com

This agreement applies when an institution uses InterCampus to process personal data on its behalf. By accepting during workspace creation, the authorised institution representative agrees on behalf of that institution.

1. Parties and roles

The institution identified in the workspace is the controller for personal data it places in the service. SSEMWANGA JOSHUA, operating InterCampus, acts as processor for that data, except where the operator independently determines purposes for limited account administration, security, billing or service communications.

2. Documented instructions and purposes

The processor will process institutional personal data only to provide, secure, support and maintain InterCampus and in accordance with the institution's documented settings and instructions, unless processing is required by law. The institution is responsible for ensuring its instructions are lawful.

3. Data subjects and data types

Data subjects may include students, parents/guardians, lecturers, administrators and other institution personnel. Data may include identity and contact details, institutional identifiers, roles, academic enrolments, profile photographs, attendance records, device identifiers and session-related location data where enabled.

4. Confidentiality and security

The processor will restrict access to authorised service operators and use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including authentication, role-based access and database security rules. The institution must secure its own accounts, devices and administrator access.

5. Sub-processors and transfers

The institution authorises use of Google Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure required to host and operate the service. The actual processing locations depend on the selected Firebase resource regions and provider architecture. The processor does not warrant that data remains in Uganda. Before production processing, the parties must assess applicable cross-border transfer rules and document any required safeguards. Material sub-processor changes will be reflected in updated service information.

6. Assistance and data-subject requests

The processor will provide reasonable assistance, within available product capabilities, to help the institution respond to requests to access, correct, export or delete institutional data. The institution remains responsible for verifying requesters and deciding whether a request must be granted.

7. Incidents

The processor will notify the institution at the contact address supplied in its workspace without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting institutional data, and will provide available information reasonably needed for the institution to assess and meet its obligations.

8. Retention, return and deletion

The institution must set and maintain its retention requirements and issue clear deletion/export instructions. On termination, the institution may request export and deletion, subject to technical feasibility, backup retention cycles and legal obligations. The processor will not decide academic retention periods for the institution.

9. Institution obligations

The institution must identify a contact for privacy matters, provide required notices, establish a lawful basis, obtain any required permissions, maintain accurate user and campus assignments, set a defensible retention period, and assess regulatory registration and international transfer requirements applicable to it.

10. Term and precedence

This agreement applies while the institution uses InterCampus for institutional personal data. If it conflicts with the general Terms on processor obligations, this agreement controls for those obligations. Material updates require renewed acceptance where appropriate.
