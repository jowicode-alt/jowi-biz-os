Institutional Data Processing Agreement

Effective 8 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-08.1 · Privacy and security contact: jowicode@gmail.com

This agreement applies when an educational institution uses InterCampus to process personal data on its behalf. By accepting during workspace creation, the authorised institution representative agrees on behalf of that institution. It supplements the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. Parties and roles

The institution identified in the workspace is the controller for personal data it places in the service. SSEMWANGA JOSHUA, operating InterCampus, acts as processor for that data, except where the operator independently determines purposes for limited account administration, security or service communications.

2. Documented instructions and purposes

The processor will process institutional personal data only to provide, secure, support and maintain InterCampus and in accordance with the institution's documented settings and instructions, unless processing is required by law. The institution is responsible for ensuring its instructions are lawful, necessary and proportionate.

3. Data subjects and data types

Data subjects may include students, parents/guardians, lecturers, administrators and other institution personnel. Data may include identity and contact details, institutional identifiers, roles, academic enrolments, profile photographs, attendance records, device identifiers and session-related location data where enabled. The institution must avoid supplying unnecessary or unrelated personal data.

4. Confidentiality and security measures

The processor will restrict service access to authorised operators and maintain reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the current service, including:

  • Firebase Authentication for account authentication; plaintext passwords are not stored by InterCampus.
  • Institution-scoped Firestore security rules intended to prevent cross-institution access.
  • Role- and campus-based authorisation for institutional records and privileged operations.
  • Session-owner restrictions for closing attendance sessions.
  • Security-focused hosting headers and no-store caching for HTML documents.
  • Automated rules tests and deployment checks for important access-control behaviour.

These controls reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. GPS and device-binding values are client/browser signals and are not tamper-proof server attestations. The institution must secure its own accounts, devices and administrator access.

5. Sub-processors and international transfers

The institution authorises use of Google Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure required to host and operate the service. Actual processing locations depend on selected Firebase resource regions and provider architecture. The processor does not warrant that data remains in Uganda. Before production processing, the parties must assess applicable cross-border transfer rules and document any required safeguards. Provider information is available in the Firebase privacy and security information.

6. Assistance and data-subject requests

The processor will provide reasonable assistance, within available product capabilities, to help the institution respond to requests to access, correct, export or delete institutional data. The institution remains responsible for verifying requesters, determining the applicable legal requirements and deciding whether a request must be granted.

7. Personal-data incidents

The processor will notify the institution's designated contact without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting institutional data and provide information reasonably available to support the institution's assessment and response. The institution must maintain a monitored incident contact and coordinate any legally required notifications.

8. Retention, return and deletion

The institution must set and maintain its retention requirements and issue clear export/deletion instructions. The service does not currently guarantee automatic deletion at the end of an institution-defined retention period. On termination, the institution may request export and deletion, subject to technical feasibility, backup retention cycles and legal obligations. The processor will not decide academic retention periods for the institution.

9. Institution obligations

The institution must identify a privacy contact, provide required notices, establish a lawful basis, obtain any required permissions, maintain accurate user and campus assignments, set a defensible retention period, assess regulatory registration and international transfer requirements, and ensure that users can raise attendance disputes and privacy requests.

10. Term and precedence

This agreement applies while the institution uses InterCampus for institutional personal data. If it conflicts with the general Terms on processor obligations, this agreement controls for those obligations. Material updates require renewed acceptance where appropriate.
